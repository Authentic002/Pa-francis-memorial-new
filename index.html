His Life and Legacy

Pa. Francis Oluremi Ademakinwa lived a life remembered for faith, prayer, humility, kindness and devotion to family. His legacy lives on in the lives he touched and the generations he helped shape.

This memorial celebrates his life, preserves his family's memories and brings loved ones together to honour him.

His Family

He was first married to the late Mrs. Racheal Morinola Ademakinwa, and later to Mrs. Iyabo Ademakinwa. His marriages were blessed with nine children:

Late Mrs. Oladunni; Mrs. Botu Ibironke; Mrs. Apaleoha Tolulope; Mrs. Koya Funmilayo; Mr. Ademakinwa Segun; Mrs. Okeme Folashade; Late Master Ademakinwa Funsho; Mrs. Iwalokun Ibiyemi; and Mrs. Amangele Oluwadamilola.
